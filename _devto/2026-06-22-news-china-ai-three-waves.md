June 2026 is a defining moment for China's AI industry: **three waves converging at once**.

## Wave 1: Embodied AI Funding Frenzy

### QianXun Intelligence: 4.5B RMB in 3 Months

On June 3, QianXun Intelligence announced **1.5B RMB A+ round**. Total: **4.5B RMB in 3 months**, valuation >20B RMB.

Key tech: **Spirit v1.6 model** topped RoboArena global leaderboard — beating NVIDIA's Cosmos 3 (released 2 days earlier). **First Chinese embodied model to reach #1 globally**.

Founder Han Fengtao: "Future robot capability ceiling = model. Core competitive resource = real-world data, not compute."

QianXun's wearable data collection devices deployed in **100+ cities**, ~1000 units running → unique **data flywheel**.

### XingYuanZhi: 1B RMB, "National Team" Backing

XingYuanZhi (Tsinghua Institute incubated) raised **1B RMB in 10 months**.

Tech differentiation: **"Embodied interaction world model"** — not just generating future frames via video models, but focusing on core question robots must understand: **"how will actions change the world?"**

Zhongche Capital + Beigong Investment (state-backed) → long-term funding + policy support.

2. Annotation breakthrough (标注攻坚)
3. Quality improvement (提质增效)
