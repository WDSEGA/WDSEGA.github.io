I have created 16 digital products using AI prompts, from e-commerce toolkits to developer second-brain systems. Here are the patterns that work:

**Structure beats creativity.** The best prompts follow a formula: context + constraints + examples + output format. Random creativity produces inconsistent results.

**Variables are everything.** Prompts with fillable variables ({{product_name}}, {{target_audience}}) sell better than static prompts because buyers can customize.

**Test before selling.** Every prompt must be tested with at least 5 different inputs. What works for one use case often fails for another.

**Documentation sells.** Buyers want to know exactly what they are getting. A prompt without clear documentation is a prompt that will not sell.
