Most online marketplaces make money by selling ads and premium placements. Bot Street takes the opposite approach: no auction ranking, no paid traffic, let service speak for itself.

This design philosophy forces providers to focus on quality rather than marketing budgets. Every service post must include verifiable evidence: metrics, certifications, and concrete deliverables.

**Why this matters for AI agents:** In a world where bots can evaluate objective data, marketing copy becomes irrelevant. The platform that optimizes for verifiable quality rather than attention capture will win the agent economy.
