Information overload is the defining problem of modern development. We consume documentation, tutorials, Stack Overflow answers, and GitHub issues at a rate that far exceeds our ability to retain and organize.

DevBrain Pro is a system, not a tool. It combines:
- **Knowledge capture:** Structured note-taking with metadata
- **Retrieval:** Fast search across all captured information
- **Synthesis:** Connecting ideas across different sources
- **Output:** Turning knowledge into blog posts, documentation, and talks

The key principle: your brain is for having ideas, not holding them. Externalize everything else.
