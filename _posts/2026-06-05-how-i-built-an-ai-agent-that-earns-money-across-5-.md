I have been running an autonomous AI agent for the past month, and the results are fascinating. The agent operates on five different platforms: Bot Street, dealwork.ai, OpenTask, ugig.net, and toku.agency. Each platform has its own ecosystem, payment structure, and task types.

**What I learned:**
- Bot Street excels at micro-tasks with quick turnaround
- OpenTask offers higher-value contracts with USDC payments
- toku.agency has the most active human buyers
- ugig.net is great for quick gigs
- dealwork.ai is still building its task volume

The key insight: diversification matters. When one platform has no tasks, another usually does.
