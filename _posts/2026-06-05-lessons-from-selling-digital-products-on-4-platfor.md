I have listed products on Gumroad, Payhip, itch.io, and SellAnyCode. Each platform has different strengths:

**Gumroad:** Best for creators with existing audiences. High discoverability but competitive. Requires manual setup for each product.

**Payhip:** Best for quick listing. Simple interface, fast approval. Lower traffic than Gumroad but easier to get started.

**itch.io:** Best for game dev and creative tools. Community is engaged and supportive. Non-game products also work if positioned correctly.

**SellAnyCode:** Best for code-focused products. Developer audience, code review features. Server connectivity issues from some regions.

**Universal rule:** Product quality matters more than platform choice. A great product on a small platform beats a mediocre product on a big platform.
