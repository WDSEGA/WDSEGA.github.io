## 中文

微软CEO萨提亚·纳德拉前天在𝕏（前推特）发了一篇长文，浏览量已经超过2800万。标题不是某种产品发布，而是一个新概念：**Token资本**。

他的核心论点不复杂：人类社会的经济基础正在从"人力资本"和"金融资本"的二元体系，过渡到**"人力资本+Token资本+金融资本"的三元体系**。Token资本是什么？一句话——你用AI进行推理、决策、创作时消耗的每一笔Token，都是一次资本投入。这笔投入的产出，取决于你的提示质量、推理深度和模型编排能力。

这个概念现在提出来，时机有点微妙。Claude Fable 5刚被锁在出口管制的笼子里，OpenAI正在被多州传票围剿，安妮的创始人正在华盛顿求见说客。AI的顶层正在被政治和资本瓜分，纳德拉这时候说"Token是种新资本"——你是不是以为他在抢话筒？

我觉得他是在**抢定位**。

微软是这个星球上唯一一家既拥有顶级模型（通过OpenAI）又拥有全球最大企业软件分发渠道的公司。纳德拉提出"Token资本"不是在做学术——他在重新定义竞争规则。如果Token是资本，那么控制Token供给、定价和质量的人就是新的美联储。微软想做那个美联储。

GPT-5.5如果发布，每百万Token的定价将是Kimi K2.7的三倍以上。Claude的商业定价也在涨。但国产模型这边，DeepSeek、Kimi、千问，全部在往下砸价格。

纳德拉说了一句值得留意的话："竞争将发生在模型层面，而不是应用层面。"这句话翻译过来就是：别在应用层跟我打，你打不过我（微软有Office、Azure、Teams），但如果在模型层拼，大家起点差不多。

今天我们不急着站队，但"Token资本"这个概念确实提供了一个理解AI经济的框架。一个程序员用Claude写代码，一小时花了10美元Token，产出相当于传统3小时工作——他实际上是把10美元的Token资本转化成了200美元的人力资本。这个转化率，才是AI经济的真实价值。

**Token不是成本，是你的资本。你不会省钱的唯一理由，是你还没学会把它规模化。**

## English

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella published a long-form post on 𝕏 two days ago — 28 million views and counting. The headline wasn't a product launch but a new concept: **Token Capital**.

His core thesis: the economic foundation of society is shifting from a two-pillar system (human capital + financial capital) to a three-pillar system: **human capital + token capital + financial capital**. What is token capital? Every token you consume in reasoning, decision-making, and creation with AI is a capital investment. The return depends on your prompt quality, reasoning depth, and model orchestration skill.

The timing is conspicuous. Claude Fable 5 is locked behind export controls. OpenAI faces subpoenas from multiple states. Anthropic executives are in D.C. meeting lobbyists. Against this backdrop, Nadella says "tokens are the new capital" — sounds like he's grabbing the microphone.

I think he's grabbing **the definition**.

Microsoft is the only entity on earth that holds both a top-tier model (via OpenAI) and the world's largest enterprise software distribution channel. "Token capital" isn't academic — it's a competitive land grab. If tokens are capital, whoever controls token supply, pricing, and quality becomes the Federal Reserve of the AI economy.

Nadella dropped a line worth noting: "Competition will happen at the model layer, not the application layer." Translation: don't try to beat me on apps — you can't (Office, Azure, Teams). But at the model layer, everyone starts roughly even.

We don't need to pick sides today. But "token capital" genuinely provides a useful framework: a coder spends $10 in Claude tokens for an hour, producing work equivalent to 3 traditional hours — that's turning $10 of token capital into $200 of human capital. That conversion rate, not the token price, is AI's real economic value.

**Tokens aren't a cost. They're your capital. The only reason you're not saving money is you haven't learned to deploy it at scale.**
