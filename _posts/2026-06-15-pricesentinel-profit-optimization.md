## 中文

我们之前介绍过PriceSentinel Pro的基础功能——监控竞品价格变化，发现降价就通知你。

但那只是第一步。

真正的定价问题是这样的：你的产品定价99元，竞品突然降到79元。你应该跟降到79吗？跟——你的毛利从50%掉到32%。不跟——流量被抢走。这个问题的答案不是"跟"或"不跟"，而是**你清楚自己的利润底线是多少**。

### 定价的三个层次

**第一层：监控。** 竞品涨了、跌了、下线了、换图了——你知道，但不知道怎么反应。PriceSentinel的监控模块就是干这个的，实时对比，差额超过阈值就告警。

**第二层：分析。** 竞品降价的时候，你的利润结构允许你跟吗？PriceSentinel的利润分析模块会同时算三笔账：当前售价下的毛利、跟价后的毛利、以及需要多少件额外销量才能抵消毛利下滑。一个产品如果跟价后需要多卖130%才能打平毛利——你大概率不该跟。

**第三层：策略。** 不是所有产品都该打价格战。PriceSentinel按产品分组做差异化策略：爆款引流品——允许跟价甚至低于成本（当获客成本算），利润主力款——守住底价不跟，滞销清仓款——低于成本出清。每一组有自己的监控规则和告警阈值。

小卖家做定价的方式：看一眼竞品→改个价格→等三天看反应→再改。这是猜，不是算。大卖家的定价方式：有数据，有模型，有底线。中间差的不是资本，是一个叫"利润意识"的东西。PriceSentinel Pro做的不是帮你盯价格——是帮你建立那个意识。

We've covered PriceSentinel Pro's basic function before — monitor competitor price changes and alert you to drops.

But that's just level one.

Here's the real pricing problem: your product is priced at ¥99. Competitor drops to ¥79. Do you match? If yes — your margin drops from 50% to 32%. If no — you lose traffic. The answer isn't "match" or "don't match." It's **knowing exactly what your margin floor is**.

### Three levels of pricing

**Level 1: Monitoring.** Competitor went up, down, offline, changed images — you know, but don't know how to respond. PriceSentinel's monitoring module handles this: real-time comparison, threshold-based alerts.

**Level 2: Analysis.** When a competitor drops price, can your profit structure absorb a match? PriceSentinel's profit module calculates three numbers simultaneously: current margin, post-match margin, and how many extra units you'd need to sell just to break even. If matching requires a 130% sales increase to maintain profit — you probably shouldn't match.

**Level 3: Strategy.** Not every product should fight a price war. PriceSentinel groups products for differentiated strategies: traffic-drivers (allow matching, even below cost — treat as customer acquisition cost), profit-core products (hold floor price, don't match), clearance inventory (sell below cost). Each group has its own monitoring rules and alert thresholds.
