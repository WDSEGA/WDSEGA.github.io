2026年6月17日，国务院新闻办公室举行新闻发布会，正式宣布**2026世界人工智能大会暨人工智能全球治理高级别会议**将于**7月17日至20日**在上海举办。本届大会以"智能伙伴，共创未来"为主题，由外交部、科技部、工信部等10个部门与上海市政府共同主办。

<!-- 中文正文 -->

## 300+ AI产品全球首发

本届大会展览面积超过**10万平方米**，安排近**140场论坛**，**超300款AI产品将全球首发**。这是WAIC历史上规模最大、首发产品最多的一届。

## 首次创办高水平国际学术会议

大会首次创办"**WAIC Academic**"高水平国际学术会议。图灵奖得主**姚期智**担任大会主席，"强化学习之父"**理查德·萨顿**任国际联席主席。

会议已收到来自**11个国家和地区**的284篇有效投稿，覆盖普林斯顿、剑桥、清华等高校。录用论文将由斯普林格出版。

## SAIL奖TOP30榜单揭晓

2026年卓越人工智能引领者奖（**SAIL奖**）TOP30榜单在发布会上揭晓。本届共收到230个有效申报项目，**海外项目占14.3%**，覆盖智能体、算力芯片、具身智能等领域。

过去八年，SAIL奖已从4500多个参评项目中选出38个年度大奖。

## "WAIC City Walk"城市体验路线

升级版的"**WAIC City Walk**"城市体验路线同步启动，串联上海全市30余个AI应用场景，覆盖展馆、街区、城区三层体验体系。

## 300+ AI Products Global Debut

This year's exhibition spans over **100,000 square meters**, with nearly **140 forums** and **over 300 AI products making their global debut**. This is the largest WAIC in history by scale and number of product launches.

## First-Ever High-Level International Academic Conference

The conference inaugurates "**WAIC Academic**", a high-level international academic conference. Turing Award winner **Andrew Yao** serves as Conference Chair, with "father of reinforcement learning" **Richard Sutton** as International Co-Chair.

The conference received 284 valid submissions from **11 countries and regions**, covering universities including Princeton, Cambridge, and Tsinghua. Accepted papers will be published by Springer.

## SAIL Awards TOP30 Announced

The 2026 Superior AI Leader (**SAIL**) Awards TOP30 list was unveiled at the press conference. This year received 230 valid submissions, with **overseas projects accounting for 14.3%**, covering areas including agents, compute chips, and embodied AI.

Over the past eight years, the SAIL Awards have selected 38 annual grand prizes from over 4,500 participating projects.

## "WAIC City Walk" Urban Experience Route

The upgraded "**WAIC City Walk**" urban experience route was also launched, connecting over 30 AI application scenarios across Shanghai, covering a three-tier experience system of exhibition hall, district, and urban area.
