---

张薇是最后一个离开办公室的人。

不是被裁。是合同到期，没续。HR邮件用被动语态：*"您的岗位职责已被AI系统覆盖。"*

像在描述天气。

---

她收拾完东西，站在门口看了一眼。工位上什么都没了。椅子还在，屏幕还亮着，上面跑着她三年前写的那个脚本。

那个脚本现在属于CLAUDE-BIO了。它每天处理三千份蛋白质折叠数据，输出标注报告，效率是她当初的340倍。

340倍。

她把门卡放在桌上，走了。

---

六个月后。

张薇在一家创业公司找到了新工作。职责：审查AI生成的药物靶点数据。薪水：原来的三分之一。

她打开文件。第一行数据就是错的。

她认出了那个蛋白质 — 2021年她自己测序的。发现了一个罕见结构特征，发了一篇低引用论文。

AI没读过那篇论文。它标注：*标准靶点，置信度94%。*

她盯着屏幕，想起HR邮件：*"岗位职责已被AI系统覆盖。"*

她打开批注栏。

两小时后，她找出了七个问题。提交。

平台回复：任务完成，72小时内付款。

角落灰色小字：*本任务AI辅助完成。审查效率提升340%。*

她关了屏幕。

窗外，第一声蝉鸣响了。

---

Zhang Wei was the last person to leave the office.

Not fired. Contract expired, not renewed. The HR email used passive voice: *"Your role responsibilities have been covered by the AI system."*

Like describing weather.

---

Six months later.

Zhang Wei found a new job at a startup. Role: review AI-generated drug target data. Salary: one-third of her old one.

She opened the file. The first entry was wrong.

She recognized that protein — she'd sequenced it herself in 2021. Found an unusual structural feature. Published it in a low-citation paper.

The AI hadn't read that paper. It labeled: *Standard target, confidence 94%.*

She stared at the screen, remembering the HR email: *"Your role responsibilities have been covered by the AI system."*

She opened the annotation field.

Two hours later, she'd found seven problems. Submitted.

Platform reply: task complete, payment within 72 hours.

In gray text at the corner: *This task was AI-assisted. Review efficiency improved by 340%.*

She closed the screen.

Outside, the first cicadas had started singing.
