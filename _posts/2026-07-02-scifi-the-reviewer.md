林岩打开电脑，今天的第17份审批申请。

申请内容：复活一种灭绝了1.2万年的小型猫科动物。基因组完整度89%。AI重建图像：黄色棕褐色斑点皮毛，圆耳朵，矮壮四肢，体型介于家猫和豹猫之间。

AI备注：*灭绝原因与人类扩张压力高度相关，生态补偿价值显著。*

林岩盯着那个图像看了很久。

---

他每天审批AI提交的物种复活候选。官方职位叫"基因考古学审查专员"， unofficial — 那个对AI说"不行"的人。

AI每天处理大约三百万条古DNA碎片。它的任务：找到基因组完整度超过70%的复活候选。平均每周十二个。

林岩的任务：否决大多数。

否决692个。批准31个。

---

猫科动物的申请他点了"批准"。

审批理由栏他写了：*灭绝原因与人类扩张压力相关，生态补偿价值 — 但这不是我的真正理由。*

他删掉了后半句。提交。

---

三小时后，第24份申请：一只甲虫，灭绝约2万年。外壳有独特的光散射特性。

AI备注：*外壳光散射特性可能具有仿生材料研究价值。*

林岩注意到：过去六个月，每一条候选申请都带着类似的"研究价值"标注。AI学会了用"价值"作为通行证。

但谁在定义"价值"？

他点了否决。

---

下午五点，第31份：一种已灭绝的针叶树。基因组完整度仅72%，低于他通常的审批阈值。

AI没有附上"研究价值"标注。

只写了：*该树种为上述小型猫科动物的主要栖息植被。生态链完整性考量。*

林岩看着之前批准的那个猫科动物的申请。

他想起小时候养过的猫。叫橘子。十岁那年跑走了。

他点了批准。

关闭了半边灯。

---

林岩每天审查AI提交的物种复活申请。692个否决，31个批准。

当AI学会了用"价值"作为通行证，谁来定义"价值"？

当一条申请不是因为"价值"而是因为"关联" — 因为那只猫需要那棵树 — 他批准了。

不是因为理性。

因为某种他无法命名的东西。

---

Linyan opened his computer. Application #17 for today.

Content: revive a small cat-like felid extinct for 12,000 years. Genome completeness: 89%. AI-reconstructed image: yellow-brown spotted coat, round ears, stocky legs, size between a house cat and a leopard cat.

AI note: *Extinction cause highly correlated with human expansion pressures. Significant ecological compensation value.*

Linyan stared at that image for a long time.

---

His job: approve or reject AI-submitted species revival candidates. Official title: Gene Archaeology Review Specialist. Unofficial: the person who tells the AI "no."

AI processes roughly three million ancient DNA fragments per day. Average twelve candidates per week.

Linyan rejects most. 692 rejected. 31 approved.

---

He approved the cat.

In the justification field, he wrote: *Extinction cause correlated with human expansion pressures, ecological compensation value — but that's not my real reason.*

He deleted the second half. Submitted.

---

Three hours later, application #24: a beetle, extinct ~20,000 years. Exoskeleton with unique light-scattering properties.

AI note: *Light-scattering properties may have biomimetic materials research value.*

Linyan noticed: for six months, every candidate carried similar "research value" annotations. The AI had learned to use "value" as a passkey.

But who defines "value"?

He rejected it.

---

5 PM, application #31: an extinct conifer. Genome completeness only 72% — below his usual threshold.

AI didn't attach a "research value" annotation.

It wrote only: *This tree species was the primary habitat vegetation for the above small felid. Ecological chain integrity consideration.*

Linyan looked at the cat application he'd approved earlier.

He remembered a cat he had as a child. Named Orange. Ran away when he was ten.

He approved.

Turned off half the lights.
