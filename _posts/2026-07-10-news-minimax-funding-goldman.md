## MiniMax获160亿港元融资CEO零薪酬承诺，高盛喊话转投中国AI价值链

2026年7月10日，两条消息同时指向同一个信号：**全球资本正在加速涌入中国AI产业链**。

### MiniMax：160亿港元，7倍认购

MiniMax完成新一轮**160亿港元融资**，吸引多家顶级国际主权基金和长线基金参与，实现**7倍认购覆盖**。这意味着想投的钱是实际融资额的7倍。

更引人注目的是创始人兼CEO闫俊杰的全员信承诺：

> 在实现AGI之前，不领取任何薪酬，并拿出个人股份激励团队及支持开源社区发展。

这不是常见的PR话术——将个人薪酬与AGI目标绑定，等于把自己置于"all in"的位置。对于一家估值已达数百亿的公司CEO来说，这个承诺的分量不轻。

### 高盛：从韩国AI切换到中国AI价值链

同一天，高盛在最新报告中建议客户**将仓位从韩国AI交易切换至"中国AI价值链"**，覆盖范围包括：

- 电力（AI数据中心供电）
- 半导体（国产芯片产业链）
- AI基础设施（服务器、散热、网络）
- AI模型（大模型公司）
- AI应用（垂直行业落地）

高盛分析师指出，中国AI行业已正式进入全球投资者视野。大规模国家支持、激增的全球需求以及结构性资本轮动，使中国AI成为当今科技领域最引人注目的增长故事之一。

### 两条消息的共同指向

MiniMax的7倍认购 + 高盛的明确喊话，传递的信号一致：**国际资本不再只是观望中国AI，而是在用真金白银下注**。

此前，全球AI投资主要集中在美国（OpenAI、Anthropic合计近千亿美元ARR）。中国AI公司融资规模长期偏低。但2026年下半年，随着国产十万卡集群落成、开源模型全球调用量第一、国产芯片自研推进，资本叙事正在发生根本转变。

值得注意的是，同日Ollama（开源AI模型运行工具）也完成了6500万美元B轮融资，月活开发者超890万，覆盖85%的财富500强企业，团队仅14人。开源AI工具的资本热度同样不减。

## MiniMax Raises HK$16B, Goldman Sachs Urges Pivot to China AI Value Chain

On July 10, 2026, two simultaneous signals pointed to the same trend: **global capital is accelerating its flow into China's AI industry chain**.

### MiniMax: HK$16B, 7x Oversubscribed

MiniMax completed a new funding round of **HK$16 billion**, attracting top international sovereign funds and long-term funds, achieving **7x oversubscription**. That means committed capital was 7 times the actual raise.

Even more striking was founder and CEO Yan Junjie's all-staff letter commitment:

> I will not draw any salary before achieving AGI, and will dedicate personal shares to incentivize the team and support open-source community development.

This is not typical PR language — binding personal compensation to the AGI goal puts him in an "all in" position. For a CEO of a company valued at tens of billions, this commitment carries real weight.

### Goldman Sachs: Switch from Korea AI to China AI Value Chain

On the same day, Goldman Sachs recommended clients **rotate positions from the Korea AI trade to the "China AI value chain,"** covering:

- Power (AI data center electricity)
- Semiconductors (domestic chip industry)
- AI infrastructure (servers, cooling, networking)
- AI models (large model companies)
- AI applications (vertical industry deployment)

Goldman analysts noted that China's AI industry has officially entered global investors' field of vision. Massive state support, surging global demand, and structural capital rotation make China AI one of the most compelling growth stories in tech today.

### The Common Signal

MiniMax's 7x oversubscription + Goldman's explicit call convey the same message: **international capital is no longer just watching China AI — it's betting with real money**.

Previously, global AI investment concentrated in the US (OpenAI and Anthropic combined nearing $100B ARR). Chinese AI company funding lagged. But in H2 2026, with domestic 100K-GPU clusters completed, open-source models topping global API call volumes, and domestic chip R&D advancing, the capital narrative is fundamentally shifting.

Notably, on the same day, Ollama (open-source AI model runner) also closed a $65M Series B, with 8.9M monthly active developers, covering 85% of Fortune 500 companies, with just a 14-person team. Open-source AI tool capital heat remains strong.
