## 中国首个全国产十万卡AI超集群曙光8000落成：从万卡到十万卡的跨越

2026年7月10日，中科曙光宣布中国首个全国产十万卡AI超集群——**曙光8000**正式落成，同步接入国家超算互联网。这标志着中国AI基础设施建设从万卡级正式迈向十万卡级部署阶段。

### 核心规格

曙光8000采用**原生超智融合架构**，关键技术指标包括：

- **全精度计算**：支持FP64至INT8全精度计算，兼顾科学计算与AI推理
- **十万卡规模**：全部采用国产加速卡，不依赖进口GPU
- **超智融合**：已完成300余项超智融合应用优化
- **领域覆盖**：覆盖大模型、机器人、汽车、创新药等二十余个领域

### 为什么这很重要

十万卡是一个分水岭。此前，全球能部署十万卡级AI集群的只有英伟达GPU体系。曙光8000的落成意味着：

1. **国产替代迈过临界点**：从"能用"到"好用"，十万卡规模验证了国产加速卡的可靠性
2. **超算与AI融合**：FP64科学计算 + INT8 AI推理在同一集群上运行，打破了超算和AI集群的壁垒
3. **供应链安全**：在全国产基础上实现十万卡，意味着即使面临极端出口管制，中国AI算力也不会停摆

### 与全球对比

OpenAI的Colossus集群、Meta的SuperCluster都在十万卡级别，但均依赖英伟达GPU。曙光8000是首个全国产十万卡集群，虽然在单卡峰值性能上可能仍有差距，但在系统级整合和供应链安全上具有战略意义。

同步接入国家超算互联网，意味着全国科研机构和企业可以按需调用曙光8000的算力，降低AI研发门槛。

### 产业影响

曙光8000的落成将加速国产大模型训练。此前，美团开源的LongCat-2.0已在五万卡国产算力集群上完成推理优化。十万卡集群的可用性，将使万亿参数模型的训练不再依赖进口算力。

## China's First Fully Domestic 100K-GPU AI Supercluster Sugon 8000 Completed

On July 10, 2026, Sugon announced the completion of China's first fully domestic 100,000-GPU AI supercluster — **Sugon 8000** — simultaneously integrated into the National Supercomputing Internet. This marks China's AI infrastructure advancing from the 10K-GPU tier to the 100K-GPU deployment stage.

### Key Specs

Sugon 8000 adopts a **native super-AI convergence architecture** with key technical indicators:

- **Full precision**: FP64 to INT8, supporting both scientific computing and AI inference
- **100K scale**: Entirely domestic accelerator cards, no imported GPUs
- **Super-AI convergence**: 300+ application optimizations completed
- **Domain coverage**: Large models, robotics, automotive, drug discovery, and 20+ fields

### Why It Matters

100,000 cards is a watershed. Previously, only NVIDIA GPU-based systems reached this scale globally. Sugon 8000 means:

1. **Domestic substitution crosses the threshold**: From "usable" to "good," 100K scale validates domestic card reliability
2. **Supercomputing-AI convergence**: FP64 scientific computing + INT8 AI inference on the same cluster, breaking the wall between supercomputers and AI clusters
3. **Supply chain security**: 100K scale on fully domestic basis means China's AI compute won't stop even under extreme export controls

Integration with the National Supercomputing Internet means research institutions and enterprises nationwide can access Sugon 8000's compute on demand, lowering AI R&D barriers.

### Industry Impact

Sugon 8000 will accelerate domestic large model training. Meituan's recently open-sourced LongCat-2.0 already completed inference optimization on a 50K domestic compute cluster. The availability of a 100K cluster means trillion-parameter model training no longer depends on imported compute.
