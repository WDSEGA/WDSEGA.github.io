# Science：AI智能体40分钟完成人类60小时实验；Cursor秘密开发办公助手Sand挑战Claude

> 2026年7月11日，两条消息同时炸场：Science期刊报道AI智能体将60小时实验压缩到40分钟，Cursor被曝秘密开发办公助手直接叫板Claude。

## 一、Science期刊：AI已经会自己做实验了

2026年7月11日凌晨，Science期刊报道了一项重大突破：AI智能体可在**40分钟内完成人类需60小时才能完成的实验工作**。

这意味着什么？

- 人类研究员：60小时 = 7.5个工作日
- AI智能体：40分钟 = 不到一个 lunch break
- **效率提升约90倍**

这项成果标志着AI从"辅助工具"向"自主科研主体"的转变。过去AI帮助人类分析数据、撰写论文；现在AI自己设计实验、执行操作、收集结果。

对科研领域的冲击是深远的：当一个AI智能体可以不间断地运行实验，研究周期从"月"压缩到"天"甚至"小时"，科研产出的速度将呈指数级增长。

## 二、Cursor秘密开发办公助手"Sand"

同一天凌晨，AI编程工具Cursor被曝秘密立项"**Sand**"项目。

目标：开发能写代码的AI办公助手，直接挑战Anthropic的Claude Cowork市场地位。

这意味着什么？

Cursor目前是AI编程工具赛道的头部选手。但它不满足于只帮程序员写代码——它想成为一个**能处理办公文档、管理任务、辅助决策的全能助手**。

竞争对手很明确：

| 维度 | Cursor Sand（曝光中） | Claude Cowork（已上线） |
|------|----------------------|------------------------|
| 定位 | AI办公助手 | AI办公协作平台 |
| 代码能力 | 强（编程工具出身） | 中等 |
| 文档处理 | 待确认 | 已支持 |
| 市场地位 | 挑战者 | 先发者 |

开发者社区反应强烈。有开发者表示："Cursor如果能把编程能力和办公助手结合，体验可能比Claude Cowork更好——毕竟它的代码理解能力是一流的。"

## 三、两个事件背后的共同趋势

这两条新闻看似无关，但指向同一个方向：**AI正在从"回答问题"进化到"执行任务"**。

- Science的实验AI：不是帮人类想实验方案，而是**自己动手做实验**
- Cursor的Sand：不是帮人类补全代码，而是**替人类完成办公任务**

这就是2026年AI行业的主旋律：**从Copilot到Autopilot**。

# Science: AI Agent Completes 60-Hour Experiment in 40 Minutes; Cursor Secretly Builds Office Assistant Sand to Challenge Claude

> July 11, 2026: Two bombshells dropped simultaneously — Science reports an AI agent compressed 60 hours of experiments into 40 minutes, and Cursor was revealed to be secretly building an office assistant to take on Claude.

## 1. Science: AI Can Now Run Its Own Experiments

On July 11, Science journal reported a breakthrough: an AI agent completed experimental work in **40 minutes that would take humans 60 hours**.

What does this mean?

- Human researcher: 60 hours = 7.5 working days
- AI agent: 40 minutes = less than a lunch break
- **~90x efficiency improvement**

This marks AI's transition from "assistance tool" to "autonomous research entity." Previously, AI helped analyze data and write papers; now it designs experiments, executes procedures, and collects results.

The impact on research is profound: when an AI agent can run experiments nonstop, research cycles compress from months to days or even hours, and scientific output accelerates exponentially.

## 2. Cursor Secretly Develops Office Assistant "Sand"

Cursor isn't satisfied with helping programmers write code. It wants to be a **full-spectrum assistant** that handles documents, manages tasks, and supports decision-making.

| Dimension | Cursor Sand (in development) | Claude Cowork (live) |
|-----------|------------------------------|----------------------|
| Position | AI office assistant | AI collaboration platform |
| Code ability | Strong (coding tool DNA) | Moderate |
| Document handling | TBD | Supported |
| Market position | Challenger | First mover |

Developer community reaction: "If Cursor can combine its coding prowess with office assistance, the experience could surpass Claude Cowork — their code understanding is top-tier."

## 3. The Common Thread

These two stories point in the same direction: **AI is evolving from "answering questions" to "executing tasks."**

- Science's experiment AI: not helping design experiments, but **running them itself**
- Cursor's Sand: not autocompleting code, but **completing office tasks for humans**

This is the defining theme of AI in 2026: **from Copilot to Autopilot**.
